Smart Camera

An on-device photography assistant. Pose detection, composition, zoom and lighting guidance run locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Requires camera access. Works offline once installed.

About Smart Camera

Everything here runs locally: camera frames are analysed in your browser with on-device models (MediaPipe Pose, face and object detection, scene classification). No frames, poses or photos are sent anywhere. No analytics. No face recognition. No biometric storage.

Photos are kept in this device's app storage until you save or share them. The original capture is never modified; filters are applied to copies only.
